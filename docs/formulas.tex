\title{Formulas of the InferMesh Gateway}

\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Raja Babu}
\IEEEauthorblockA{IIT (ISM) Dhanbad}}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
InferMesh is an OpenAI-compatible gateway that sends each request to an LLM
worker likely to hold the prompt prefix in KV cache, and that keeps serving
when a worker fails or rate-limits. Nothing in the live path is a new
theorem: each identity is a published special case (SGLang cache-aware
routing, Azar--Mitzenmacher power of two choices, the SRE 10\% retry budget,
Envoy linear ejection time, RFC~9110 \texttt{Retry-After}) or is marked
\emph{assumed}. This note records the identities the code computes, the
defaults, and the three places the gateway differs from the cited source
(miss $\to$ P2C, ETIF mix $\alpha=0.6$, DistServe $C_i$ in the replay only).
\end{abstract}

\begin{IEEEkeywords}
LLM serving, prefix cache, power of two choices, retry budget, outlier ejection
\end{IEEEkeywords}

\section{Introduction}

A transformer engine reuses a prompt prefix only if the next request lands
on the worker that still holds those KV blocks. Independent uniform
assignment wastes that reuse; sticky assignment pins load. Failures and
429s add a second problem: unbounded retries turn a short outage into a
metastable collapse~\cite{bronson2021}.

InferMesh sits between OpenAI-format clients and OpenAI-compatible backends.
On each request it (i)~matches the prompt in a character radix trie,
(ii)~picks a worker, (iii)~admits at most one retry from a credit ledger,
(iv)~ejects a worker that posts consecutive 5xx. The predicates and
constants are those of SGLang's cache-aware router~\cite{sglang},
Azar \emph{et al.}~\cite{azar1999} with Mitzenmacher's supermarket
model~\cite{mitzenmacher2001}, Google SRE Chapter~21~\cite{sre2016}, and
Envoy outlier detection~\cite{envoy-outlier}. Replay service times follow
the \emph{form} of DistServe Appendix~A~\cite{zhong2024} with unpublished
constants.

This is a specification of the running code, not a claim of a new
algorithm. A line marked \emph{assumed} is used and has no published
derivation. Simulator-only identities are confined to
Section~\ref{sec:replay}. Defaults below are the shipped values.

\paragraph{Notation.}
Pool $W$ for a model: the breaker admits the worker and waiters $<Q$.
Load $L(w)$ is requests in flight. Prompt string $p$. Match depth $d$.
Attempts $A=2$.

\section{Prefix}

\[
p=\operatorname{join}(\mathsf{role}:\mathsf{content})
\]
over \texttt{messages}, newline-separated
(\texttt{extract\_prefix}). Completions use the raw \texttt{prompt}
string. Embeddings skip the trie.

\section{Token reserve (ETIF)}

Heuristic, not a tokenizer. Let $c$ be the sum of \texttt{content} lengths
in \texttt{messages} (roles are not counted).
\[
\hat{n}=\max\bigl(1,\lfloor c/4\rfloor\bigr)+\mathtt{max\_tokens}
\]
(or $256$ if the client omits \texttt{max\_tokens}). Set
\texttt{etif\_count\_output\_tokens} to false to drop the second term.
Reserved until the response ends. $c$ is not $|p|$: the trie string
includes \texttt{role:}.

\section{Selection}

\emph{Problem.} Send $p$ to a worker that already holds a long prefix of
$p$, unless that pins load.

SGLang cache-aware (\texttt{sgl-model-gateway}) uses CLI defaults
cache-threshold $0.3$, balance-abs-threshold $64$,
balance-rel-threshold $1.5$; load is in-flight count. Imbalanced iff both
\[
\max L-\min L>64
\quad\text{and}\quad
\max L>1.5\min L.
\]
Otherwise a hit iff there exist live tenants of the longest match with
\[
d\ge d_{\min}
\quad\text{and}\quad
d>0.3|p|,
\]
where $d_{\min}$ is \texttt{kv\_match\_min\_chars}. Then pick
$\arg\min L$ among those tenants.

Here: the same predicates. On miss \emph{or} imbalance, power-of-two
choices, not SGLang's min-load (random tie-break). Match rate in SGLang is
$d/|p|$ in characters; same here. Insert of $p$ on dispatch
(\texttt{insert\_on\_dispatch=true}), as SGLang inserts at selection.

\section{Power of two choices}

\begin{theorem}[Azar--Broder--Karlin--Upfal~\cite{azar1999}]
$n$ balls, $n$ bins, each ball into the least loaded of $d\ge 2$ uniform
bins: max load $(\log\log n)/(\log d)+O(1)$ w.h.p. For $d=1$ it is
$\Theta(\log n/\log\log n)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Supermarket~\cite{mitzenmacher2001}]
Poisson arrivals rate $\lambda n$, $\lambda<1$, exponential service;
$d=2$ cuts expected wait exponentially versus $d=1$.
\end{theorem}

Here: $d=2$. Sample without replacement, weights $=$ worker
\texttt{weight}. Compare scores, not raw queue length. The theorems apply
only while the score is a live queue; they do not cover a stale or mixed
score.

\section{Score}

Let $T(w)$ be tokens in flight, $U(w)\in[0,1]$ engine KV util, $q(w)$
engine wait depth. Write
$e=\min(T/S,1)$ and $q'=\min(q/q_0,1)$. Then
\[
s(w)=
\begin{cases}
e & \text{openai or stale $U$},\\
\alpha e+\beta q'+(1-\alpha-\beta)U & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
\]
Defaults: $\alpha=0.6$, $\beta=0$, $q_0=8$, $S=20\cdot 1024$ (or
\texttt{etif\_scale}). Stale means older than $3$ poll intervals.
$\alpha=0.6$ is \emph{assumed} (no published mix). With $\beta=0$ the vLLM
term is $0.4U$.

\section{Trie}

Character radix (Morrison PATRICIA~\cite{morrison1968}): one child per
first character, split on LCP. Tenants stamped on every node of an insert.
Match may stop inside an edge.

TTL $300\,\mathrm{s}$ per attribution. Cap $2^{26}$ characters per worker
(SGLang \texttt{max\_tree\_size}). Over cap: drop LRU leaves until $0.9$
of the cap (\emph{assumed} headroom; SGLang uses a periodic node LRU).
LCP length: binary search on slices, $O(\log n)$ Python steps, $O(n)$
bytes compared.

\section{Retry budget}

SRE~\cite{sre2016}: retry while the retry ratio stays below 10 percent;
amplification at most about $1.1$ versus about $3$ with a per-request cap
of three. Envoy \texttt{RetryBudget}: default 20 percent of
active+pending, floor \texttt{min\_retry\_concurrency=3}.

Here: credit ledger, mode \texttt{static},
\begin{align*}
c &\leftarrow \min(c_{\max},\,c+0.10),\\
\rho &= \rho_0(1-\alpha_{\rho}h),
\quad \rho_0=1,\;\alpha_{\rho}=0.
\end{align*}
Admit a retry iff $c\ge\rho$; then $c\leftarrow c-\rho$. With
$\alpha_{\rho}=0$, $\rho=1$: one retry per ten requests in the mean, burst
$c_0=3$, cap $c_{\max}=10$. $h=d_w/|p|$ for the fallback worker; unused
while $\alpha_{\rho}=0$.

Mode \texttt{headroom} (\emph{assumed}, off): deposit
$\max\bigl(0,\min(R/(\rho_0\lambda)-1,1)\bigr)$ once $\lambda$ is known
over a window of at least $1\,\mathrm{s}$. Local reroutes (open breaker,
full queue) skip the ledger. Hard cap $A=2$ attempts, distinct workers.
Retry only before the first byte, on 429, 5xx, or transport. Timeouts are
not retried.

\section{Circuit breaker}

Envoy outlier~\cite{envoy-outlier}: \texttt{consecutive\_5xx=5}. Real
ejection time equals \texttt{base\_ejection\_time} times consecutive
ejections, capped by \texttt{max\_ejection\_time}. Defaults
$30\,\mathrm{s}$ and $300\,\mathrm{s}$.
\[
T(k)=\min(T_0 k,\,T_{\max}),\qquad T_0=30,\; T_{\max}=300.
\]
First open $k=1\Rightarrow T=30\,\mathrm{s}$. Success sets $k=0$. Explicit
\texttt{Retry-After} cool-down does not increment $k$.

States: CLOSED $\to$ OPEN after 5 consecutive 5xx (not 429) $\to$
HALF\_OPEN after $T$, one probe. 429 opens for the header delay,
unmultiplied.

Not taken from Envoy: \texttt{max\_ejection\_percent} (default 10 percent);
the interval that decrements the multiplier while healthy (here a success
zeros $k$).

\section{Cooldown and queue}

RFC~9110 §10.2.3~\cite{rfc9110}:
\texttt{Retry-After} is an HTTP-date or delay-seconds.
Invalid $\Rightarrow$ ignore. Else the largest vendor reset
(\texttt{x-ratelimit-reset-*}, Anthropic RFC~3339 / epoch). Cap
$300\,\mathrm{s}$. Never $\infty$.

Semaphore $C=20$ in-flight per worker. Waiters $\le Q=40$, wait
$\le 30\,\mathrm{s}$, else 503 with \texttt{Retry-After}. Routable while
waiters $<Q$.

\section{Replay benches (not the gateway)}
\label{sec:replay}

Prefill time, DistServe~\cite{zhong2024} Appendix~A \emph{form}
$T=C_1(\cdots)+C_2(\cdots)+C_3$, constants fitted per model/GPU and
unpublished:
\[
t_{\mathrm{prefill}}(u)=c_1 u+c_2 u^2+c_3
\]
with $c_1=4\cdot10^{-5}$, $c_2=3\cdot10^{-9}$, $c_3=0.02$, ITL
$0.02\,\mathrm{s}$ \emph{assumed}. Claim policy order, not seconds.

SLO in the replay metrics: TTFT $0.25\,\mathrm{s}$, TPOT
$0.10\,\mathrm{s}$ (DistServe Table~1, OPT-13B chatbot).
\texttt{docs/benchmarks.md} reports a $2\,\mathrm{s}$ TTFT cut on the long
Mooncake prompts. Retry sim: Bronson \emph{et al.}~\cite{bronson2021}
§2.1, capacity 300~QPS, offered 280~QPS.

\section{Conclusion}

The live gateway is a product of published special cases. Cache hit and
imbalance are SGLang's CLI predicates; $d=2$ sampling is Azar--Mitzenmacher;
the retry ledger is SRE's 10 percent ratio with $\rho=1$; ejection time is
Envoy's $T=T_0 k$ with $T_0=30\,\mathrm{s}$ and cap $300\,\mathrm{s}$;
\texttt{Retry-After} is RFC~9110.

Three extras remain and are labelled as such: P2C on miss (SGLang uses
min-load), the ETIF mix $\alpha=0.6$, and DistServe $C_i$ in the replay
only. $\alpha_{\rho}$ and headroom mode stay off. Those constants are not
fitted here.
