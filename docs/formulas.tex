\title{Formulas of the InferMesh Gateway}

\author{Raja Babu}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Prefix KV is reused only if the next request is assigned to a replica that
still holds those blocks. That assignment is SGLang's cache-aware
predicates (match $>0.3$, imbalance $64$ and $1.5$). A miss is
GREEDY$(d{=}2)$ of Azar \emph{et al.} Retries are admitted from an SRE
$10\%$ credit ledger. A consecutive-5xx worker is ejected for
$T=T_0 k$ (Envoy), $T_0=30\,\mathrm{s}$, cap $300\,\mathrm{s}$.
Retry-After is RFC~9110. Three unpublished extras are marked
\emph{assumed}: P2C on miss, ETIF weight $\alpha=0.6$, DistServe $C_i$ in
replay only. Defaults are those of the running code.
\end{abstract}

\begin{IEEEkeywords}
LLM serving, prefix cache, power of two choices, retry budget, outlier ejection
\end{IEEEkeywords}

\section{Introduction}

Let $p$ be a prompt and $W$ the set of replicas that still accept work.
A transformer reuses KV for a prefix of $p$ only on a replica that holds
those blocks~\cite{sglang}. Uniform random assignment ignores the cache;
sticky assignment pins $L(w)$. Independently, unbounded retries after 5xx
or 429 amplify a short outage into Bronson metastability~\cite{bronson2021}.

InferMesh maps each request through four published special cases: SGLang
cache-aware selection~\cite{sglang}; GREEDY$(2)$~\cite{azar1999},
\cite{mitzenmacher2001}; SRE's $10\%$ retry ratio~\cite{sre2016}; Envoy
$T=T_0 k$~\cite{envoy-outlier}. Cool-down headers follow
RFC~9110~\cite{rfc9110}. Replay service times use the DistServe
Appendix~A \emph{form}~\cite{zhong2024} with unpublished $C_i$.

A line marked \emph{assumed} is executed and has no published derivation.
Simulator-only identities are in Section~\ref{sec:replay}. Defaults are
the shipped values.

\noindent\textit{Notation.}
Pool $W$ for a model: the breaker admits the worker and waiters $<Q$.
Load $L(w)$ is requests in flight. Prompt string $p$. Match depth $d$.
Attempts $A=2$.

\section{Prefix}

\[
p=\operatorname{join}(\mathsf{role}:\mathsf{content})
\]
over chat messages, newline-separated. Completions use the raw prompt
string. Embeddings skip the trie.

\section{Token reserve (ETIF)}

Heuristic, not a tokenizer. Let $c$ be the sum of content lengths in the
messages (roles are not counted), and let $N_{\mathrm{out}}$ be the
client's \texttt{max\_tokens} (or $256$ if omitted).
\[
\hat{n}=\max\bigl(1,\lfloor c/4\rfloor\bigr)+N_{\mathrm{out}}.
\]
Drop $N_{\mathrm{out}}$ when \texttt{etif\_count\_output\_tokens} is
false. Reserved until the response ends. $c$ is not $|p|$: the trie
string includes the role labels.

\section{Selection}

\emph{Problem.} Send $p$ to a worker that already holds a long prefix of
$p$, unless that pins load.

SGLang cache-aware uses CLI defaults cache-threshold $0.3$,
balance-abs-threshold $64$, balance-rel-threshold $1.5$; load is
in-flight count. Imbalanced iff both
\[
\max L-\min L>64
\quad\text{and}\quad
\max L>1.5\min L.
\]
Otherwise a hit iff there exist live tenants of the longest match with
\[
d\ge d_{\min}
\quad\text{and}\quad
d>0.3|p|,
\]
where $d_{\min}$ is the configured character floor. Then pick
$\arg\min L$ among those tenants.

Here: the same predicates. On miss \emph{or} imbalance, power-of-two
choices, not SGLang's min-load (random tie-break). Match rate in SGLang is
$d/|p|$ in characters; same here. Insert of $p$ on dispatch, as SGLang
inserts at selection.

\section{Power of two choices}

\begin{theorem}[Azar--Broder--Karlin--Upfal~\cite{azar1999}]
$n$ balls, $n$ bins, each ball into the least loaded of $d\ge 2$ uniform
bins: max load $(\log\log n)/(\log d)+O(1)$ w.h.p. For $d=1$ it is
$\Theta(\log n/\log\log n)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Supermarket~\cite{mitzenmacher2001}]
Poisson arrivals rate $\lambda n$, $\lambda<1$, exponential service;
$d=2$ cuts expected wait exponentially versus $d=1$.
\end{theorem}

Here: $d=2$. Sample without replacement, proportional to worker weight.
Compare scores, not raw queue length. The theorems apply only while the
score is a live queue; they do not cover a stale or mixed score.

\section{Score}

Let $T(w)$ be tokens in flight, $U(w)\in[0,1]$ engine KV util, $q(w)$
engine wait depth. Write $e=\min(T/S,1)$ and
$q'=\min(q/q_0,1)$. Then
\begin{equation}
s(w)=
\begin{cases}
e & \text{openai or stale }U,\\
\alpha e+\beta q'+(1-\alpha-\beta)U & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
Defaults: $\alpha=0.6$, $\beta=0$, $q_0=8$, $S=20\cdot 1024$. Stale means
older than $3$ poll intervals. $\alpha=0.6$ is \emph{assumed} (no
published mix). With $\beta=0$ the vLLM term is $0.4U$.

\section{Trie}

Character radix (Morrison PATRICIA~\cite{morrison1968}): one child per
first character, split on LCP. Tenants stamped on every node of an insert.
Match may stop inside an edge.

TTL $300\,\mathrm{s}$ per attribution. Cap $2^{26}$ characters per worker.
Over cap: drop LRU leaves until $0.9$ of the cap (\emph{assumed} headroom;
SGLang uses a periodic node LRU). LCP length: binary search on slices,
$O(\log n)$ Python steps, $O(n)$ bytes compared.

\section{Retry budget}

SRE~\cite{sre2016}: retry while the retry ratio stays below 10 percent;
amplification at most about $1.1$ versus about $3$ with a per-request cap
of three. Envoy RetryBudget: default 20 percent of active+pending, floor
concurrency $3$.

Here: credit ledger, mode static,
\begin{align}
c &\leftarrow \min(c_{\max},\,c+0.10),\\
\rho &= \rho_0(1-\alpha_{\rho}h),
\quad \rho_0=1,\;\alpha_{\rho}=0.
\end{align}
Admit a retry iff $c\ge\rho$; then $c\leftarrow c-\rho$. With
$\alpha_{\rho}=0$, $\rho=1$: one retry per ten requests in the mean, burst
$c_0=3$, cap $c_{\max}=10$. $h=d_w/|p|$ for the fallback worker; unused
while $\alpha_{\rho}=0$.

Mode headroom (\emph{assumed}, off): deposit
$\max\bigl(0,\min(R/(\rho_0\lambda)-1,1)\bigr)$ once $\lambda$ is known
over a window of at least $1\,\mathrm{s}$. Local reroutes (open breaker,
full queue) skip the ledger. Hard cap $A=2$ attempts, distinct workers.
Retry only before the first byte, on 429, 5xx, or transport. Timeouts are
not retried.

\section{Circuit breaker}

Envoy outlier~\cite{envoy-outlier}: five consecutive 5xx. Real ejection
time equals base ejection time times consecutive ejections, capped by max
ejection time. Defaults $30\,\mathrm{s}$ and $300\,\mathrm{s}$.
\[
T(k)=\min(T_0 k,\,T_{\max}),\qquad T_0=30,\; T_{\max}=300.
\]
First open $k=1\Rightarrow T=30\,\mathrm{s}$. Success sets $k=0$. Explicit
Retry-After cool-down does not increment $k$.

States: CLOSED $\to$ OPEN after 5 consecutive 5xx (not 429) $\to$
HALF-OPEN after $T$, one probe. 429 opens for the header delay,
unmultiplied.

Not taken from Envoy: max ejection percent (default 10 percent); the
interval that decrements the multiplier while healthy (here a success
zeros $k$).

\section{Cooldown and queue}

RFC~9110 §10.2.3~\cite{rfc9110}: Retry-After is an HTTP-date or
delay-seconds. Invalid $\Rightarrow$ ignore. Else the largest vendor
reset (reset headers, Anthropic RFC~3339 / epoch). Cap
$300\,\mathrm{s}$. Never $\infty$.

Semaphore $C=20$ in-flight per worker. Waiters $\le Q=40$, wait
$\le 30\,\mathrm{s}$, else 503 with Retry-After. Routable while
waiters $<Q$.

\section{Replay benches (not the gateway)}
\label{sec:replay}

Prefill time, DistServe~\cite{zhong2024} Appendix~A \emph{form}
$T=C_1(\cdots)+C_2(\cdots)+C_3$, constants fitted per model/GPU and
unpublished:
\[
t_{\mathrm{prefill}}(u)=c_1 u+c_2 u^2+c_3
\]
with $c_1=4\cdot10^{-5}$, $c_2=3\cdot10^{-9}$, $c_3=0.02$, ITL
$0.02\,\mathrm{s}$ \emph{assumed}. Claim policy order, not seconds.

SLO in the replay metrics: TTFT $0.25\,\mathrm{s}$, TPOT
$0.10\,\mathrm{s}$ (DistServe Table~1, OPT-13B chatbot). Benchmarks
report a $2\,\mathrm{s}$ TTFT cut on the long Mooncake prompts. Retry
sim: Bronson \emph{et al.}~\cite{bronson2021} §2.1, capacity 300~QPS,
offered 280~QPS.

\section{Conclusion}

Hit and imbalance are SGLang's CLI predicates. Sampling is GREEDY$(2)$.
The retry ledger is SRE $10\%$ with $\rho=1$. Ejection is Envoy
$T=T_0 k$, $T_0=30\,\mathrm{s}$, cap $300\,\mathrm{s}$. Cool-down is
RFC~9110.

Assumed, and left unfitted: P2C on miss (SGLang min-load),
$\alpha=0.6$, DistServe $C_i$ in replay. $\alpha_{\rho}$ and headroom
mode remain off.
